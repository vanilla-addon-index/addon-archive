## [1.0.0]

### Added
- Announcer for failed taunts, used taunts and defensive cooldowns, with a Main Tank mode.
- Nameplate colouring that shows who each mob is attacking, with automatic tank detection and a per-tank colour.
- Full in-game configuration menu and minimap button, opened with `/tks`.
- English and French localisation with automatic client language detection.
- pfUI and ShaguPlates nameplate integration.

Trailblazer is a lightweight Turtle-addon that helps you manage and summon your favorite mounts more easily.

With this addon, you can:

Select your favorite mounts by checking boxes in a simple UI.

Use the /trailblazer command to summon one of your selected favorites at random.

Favorites are saved per character and persist between sessions.

How to use:

Type /trailblazer fav in chat to open the configuration window.

Check the mounts you want to mark as favorites.

Close the window — your selections are saved automatically.

Use /trailblazer (Create macro) anytime to summon a random favorite mount.

Enjoy faster mounting with your chosen favorites — brought to you by Goldbart.
